# Slim Media Folders support policy

Slim Media Folders is maintained by Ramesh Dura. Product information is available at [Slim Media Folders](https://slim-media-folders-site.vercel.app/). For support, see the [support and FAQ section](https://slim-media-folders-site.vercel.app/#faq).

## What support covers

Support covers installation, activation, documented features, compatibility questions, and reproducible defects in the current release. Include the WordPress version, PHP version, plugin version, active theme, relevant plugins, and exact steps to reproduce a problem. Never send passwords, API keys, database dumps, or private media.

## Response and maintenance

Support is best-effort and dependent on the information supplied. Security reports should be sent privately through the product website rather than posted publicly. Bug fixes may be delivered in a maintenance release; compatibility with third-party plugins can change when those plugins change their APIs.

## Refunds

If a paid download is offered, refund requests may be submitted within 14 days of purchase when the product cannot perform its documented core function and reasonable troubleshooting has not resolved the issue. Refunds are not guaranteed for incompatibilities caused by unsupported software, modified WordPress core, or failure to provide the information needed to investigate. Applicable consumer law always takes precedence.
